## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Physical corpus has one explicit native owner
The seven original physical test functions SHALL live only in the explicit native proof owner with unchanged function, decorator, guard and parameter ASTs.

#### Scenario: physical-corpus-has-one-explicit-native-owner
- **WHEN** the preserved corpus is partitioned
- **THEN** all seven definitions remain exact and are absent from ordinary unit discovery

### Requirement: Portable tests and PID consumers remain intact
Portable test definitions, their collected identities and the existing request helper SHALL remain in ordinary discovery; the frozen PID sibling and shared helper SHALL remain byte-identical.

#### Scenario: portable-tests-and-pid-consumers-remain-intact
- **WHEN** ownership is changed
- **THEN** portable inventory and genuine PID alias consumers remain intact

### Requirement: Portable selection excludes physical proof
The actual portable selector SHALL select retained portable tests and SHALL exclude the explicit physical owner without skip-to-pass conversion.

#### Scenario: portable-selection-excludes-physical-proof
- **WHEN** targeted portable selection sees both owners
- **THEN** only portable paths are selected and physical-only selection fails empty

### Requirement: Acceptance requires physical proof before receipt
Candidate acceptance SHALL invoke the physical proof unconditionally inside the admitted candidate runtime before writing a current receipt, including local mode; native_execution SHALL be required by existing receipt validation.

#### Scenario: acceptance-requires-physical-proof-before-receipt
- **WHEN** a candidate or local acceptance is attempted
- **THEN** missing or failed physical proof prevents a success receipt and its required check

### Requirement: Existing matrix schedules every proof input
All affected proof owners, corpus fixture and adapter SHALL schedule the unchanged nine OS/ABI execute cells with the existing 60-minute ceiling and required acceptance invocation.

#### Scenario: existing-matrix-schedules-every-proof-input
- **WHEN** a proof input changes
- **THEN** the existing native matrix runs without losing prior gates

### Requirement: Fixture admission preserves delivered bytes
The operation SHALL admit exact delivered normal bytes and a separately built signed private stalled fixture with real provenance metadata before collection; the private stall SHALL stop then pause before the unique READY declaration.

#### Scenario: fixture-admission-preserves-delivered-bytes
- **WHEN** normal and stalled roots are prepared
- **THEN** hash/signature/platform admission succeeds or execution refuses before collection

### Requirement: Complete actual outcomes gate success
The operation SHALL require all 702 unique original R100 identities and passing outcomes, rejecting nonzero exit, skipped, xfail, failed, errored, missing, duplicate, extra, empty or malformed results, and SHALL retain bounded actual JUnit and summary evidence tied to source/job/cell.

#### Scenario: complete-actual-outcomes-gate-success
- **WHEN** the serial physical invocation completes
- **THEN** only exact complete actual success permits receipt writing; negative controls never qualify

### Requirement: Timeout cleanup owns only its process group
Execution SHALL use a bounded owned process group, terminate and kill only that group on timeout, reap its child and observe bounded group disappearance; timeout or incomplete cleanup SHALL always fail without receipt.

#### Scenario: timeout-cleanup-owns-only-its-process-group
- **WHEN** the proof or fixture build exceeds its bound
- **THEN** owned descendants are cleaned up without signaling observational PIDs or unrelated processes and acceptance fails


### Requirement: Customer entry does not require candidate proof imports
The acceptance CLI SHALL start and display help without a developer source path or an importable code-review namespace. Customer mode SHALL retain its separate installed-CLI workflow. Candidate acceptance SHALL still require physical proof inside the admitted runtime before project preparation and receipt writing.

#### Scenario: customer-entry-does-not-require-candidate-proof-imports
- **WHEN** the acceptance CLI starts without developer import paths and the code-review namespace is unavailable
- **THEN** help and customer dispatch remain available while candidate proof remains mandatory
