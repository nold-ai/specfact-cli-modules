## Conservative process-group observation during exit

A real cleanup trace found that an owned group accepted TERM or KILL, its Popen poll still returned None, and the immediate signal-zero probe returned EPERM before the same owned child was reaped. Apple XNU's group iteration filters zombie members and can return EPERM for an existing group with no eligible members. This published source supports an exit-window explanation; it is not an exact-host kernel identity claim.

A permission-denied signal-zero observation must remain present or unknown, never absent. Cleanup may continue observing within its existing two five-second intervals, and only ESRCH establishes group disappearance. Denied TERM or KILL must still propagate as failure. Persistent uncertainty must never qualify success. No deadline, repetition, signal authority or receipt gate changes.
